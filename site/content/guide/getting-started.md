title: New player guide
summary: Your first hour as a Champion of Clara Mundi.
---

## 1. Join the world

Launch the Clara Mundi client, create an account if needed, and make a character. You will enter the default map (often **Greenwood**) near a safe spawn.

## 2. Learn the ground

- Walk to a **save point** and interact to bind your return location.
- Talk to a **job changer** when you are ready to commit to a starting path.
- Open the in-game menus for inventory, jobs, and keybinds.

## 3. Fight when you are ready

Hostile creatures patrol regions outside sanctuaries. Early battles teach your combat mode — some maps are realtime, others use Ordo (ATB). Defeat returns you to your save point.

## 4. Grow into the Mandate

- Level your **main job**; unlock a **subjob** once your main reaches the required level.
- Travel roads between maps when exits are clear.
- Check this site’s **News** and **Status** before long sessions.

The Presence is seasonal. Train while the roads are quiet.
