## Movement

- Use your bound **move** keys (defaults: WASD or arrow keys) to walk the overworld and house interiors.
- Facing left/right follows the last horizontal input.

## Interact

- Press your **interact** bind near save crystals, job changers, camps, and house doors.
- Save points set your return location after defeat.

## Combat

- Walking within aggro range of hostile wildlife — or attacking it — starts combat right in the overworld; nearby hostiles may assist their ally.
- The **hotbar** has 24 slots (1–8, Ctrl+1–8, Shift+1–8) shared by field skills, combat skills, and items. Press **Shift** alone to dodge — it costs stamina and works in and out of combat.
- Hotbar skills and targeting depend on your jobs.

## Social

- Party invites, friends, and chat live in the desktop client HUD — not on this site.
