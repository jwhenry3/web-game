# Anthropic Corporation

Anthropic is a prominent AI research company, known for its commitment to developing safe and reliable large language models.

## Key Focus Areas
The company places significant emphasis on 'Constitutional AI,' a method of training LLMs based on a set of written principles (a constitution) rather than purely human feedback. This aims to make the model's outputs more predictable, aligned, and less prone to generating harmful or undesirable content.

## Models
They are known for their Claude series of models, which are highly regarded for their nuanced understanding and constitutional guardrails.
