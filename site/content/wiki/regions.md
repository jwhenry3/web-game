Clara Mundi is held by three great regions under the **Champion Mandate**. Each month they send Champions to thin the Presence before towns and roads break.

## Verdant heartlands

Greenwood and the canopy roads — starting ground for many Champions. Forests, sanctuaries, and the long walk toward colder frontiers.

## Frost marches

Northwatch, Frostkeep, and the ice hollows. Paths harden; wildlife turns sooner when the Tide is high.

## Tide courts

Coastal cities and wharfs — brine, cliff havens, and the trade that keeps inland towns fed.
