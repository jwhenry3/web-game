## Movement

- Use your bound **move** keys (defaults: WASD or arrow keys) to walk the overworld and house interiors.
- Facing left/right follows the last horizontal input.

## Interact

- Press your **interact** bind near save crystals, job changers, camps, and house doors.
- Save points set your return location after defeat.

## Combat

- Bumping hostile wildlife or accepting a battle invite starts combat on that map’s ruleset (**realtime** or **Ordo/ATB**).
- Hotbar skills and targeting depend on your jobs and the active combat plugin.

## Social

- Party invites, friends, and chat live in the desktop client HUD — not on this site.
